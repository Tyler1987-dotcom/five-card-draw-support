Welcome to the official support page for Five Card Draw Statistics.

About the Game

Play classic five-card draw poker against an in-game opponent. Exchange cards, reveal your hands, and track your game statistics and best poker hand.

This is a single-player card game for entertainment. It does not involve real-money betting or gambling.

How to Play

  1. Start a new hand and receive five cards.
  2. Choose which cards to exchange, keeping at least one original card.
  3. Draw replacement cards and reveal the final hands.
  4. Compare the hands using standard poker hand rankings.
  5. View your statistics and best hand in the profile section.
