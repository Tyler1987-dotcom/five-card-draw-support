Privacy Policy

Five Card Draw Statistics

Effective date: October 8, 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Five Card Draw Statistics, developed by Tyler McQuade, handles information when you use the app.

Information We Collect

The app does not require an account or ask you to provide personal information. Game statistics and progress are stored locally on your device for use within the app.

The app does not send your game statistics to a developer server. We do not collect or maintain a database of your personal information through the app.

Tracking and Advertising

The app is not designed to track you across other apps or websites and does not use your personal information for targeted advertising.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information collected through the app because the app is not designed to collect personal information.

Data Retention and Deletion

Game statistics stored locally remain on your device until they are removed by the app or its data is deleted. Uninstalling the app may remove locally stored data. Device backups and operating-system settings may affect whether data can be restored.

Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the app's features or privacy practices change. Updates will be posted on this page.

Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, please use the Five Card Draw Statistics support page on GitHub .
